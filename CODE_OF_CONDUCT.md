# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## What that means here

**Expected:** being welcoming to people whose experience differs from yours,
accepting constructive feedback gracefully, giving it kindly, and focusing on
what is best for readers of the app.

**Not acceptable:** harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks,
publishing others' private information, sustained disruption, or unwelcome
sexual attention.

## A note specific to this project

This project makes editorial judgements — which outlets to carry and which to
leave out — and reasonable people disagree about them. Those disagreements are
welcome and the reasoning is documented so it can be argued with.

Argue about the outlet: its ownership, funding, corrections record, editorial
independence. Do not turn it into an argument about the politics of whoever
raised it. A discussion that becomes about contributors rather than about
journalism will be closed.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer via the email on their GitHub profile. Reports
are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions
that breach this document, and may ban repeat offenders.
